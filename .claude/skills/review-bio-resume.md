# Skill: Review Bio & Resume

**When to use:** Before committing any update to `BIO.md` or `RESUME.md`.

**What to check:**
1. `BIO.md` is 150–200 words (count with a word-count tool, not by eye).
2. Every resume bullet leads with an action verb and includes a number where possible (placements, %, headcount, timeline) rather than a vague claim.
3. No confidential or overly specific employer data beyond what's already public/appropriate for a portfolio.
4. Consistent formatting: one blank line between Markdown sections, consistent header levels, no leftover typos from source drafts.
5. If AI helped draft or edit the content, the disclosure line is present at the bottom: *"Drafted with help from Claude (Anthropic, 2026); reviewed and edited by me."*

**Output:** A short list of specific fixes needed (quote the line, state the fix) rather than a general "looks good" — per my direct feedback preference in `AGENTS.md`.
