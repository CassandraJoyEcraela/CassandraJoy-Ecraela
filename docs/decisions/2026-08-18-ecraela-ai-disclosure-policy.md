# Decision: Disclose AI assistance on portfolio content

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Decided by:** Cassandra Joy Ecraela

## Decision

Any content in this repo that Claude (or another AI tool) helped draft or edit — starting with `BIO.md` and `RESUME.md` — carries a visible disclosure line: *"Drafted with help from Claude (Anthropic, 2026); reviewed and edited by me."* I review and personally approve the content before it's committed; the AI drafts, I decide.

## Why

The course onboarding instructions require disclosure when AI assists with portfolio content. Beyond compliance, it's honest: I used Claude to speed up formatting and wording while I was still learning Git, but the facts, judgment calls, and final wording approval are mine.

## What would reverse it

If I stop using AI assistance for a given file, I'd remove the disclosure line from that file specifically (not the whole policy). If the course drops the disclosure requirement, I'd keep the practice anyway for transparency, but would note that in an updated memo rather than silently changing it.
