# Marginal Analysis

Capability folder. What goes here: the engagements where I used marginal analysis (comparing the added benefit of one more unit against its added cost) to make or support a decision.

exercised in: [`docs/briefs/perfect-competition-brief.md`](../../docs/briefs/perfect-competition-brief.md), [`capabilities/marginal-analysis/perfect-competition-spec.md`](perfect-competition-spec.md), [`capabilities/marginal-analysis/perfect-competition-model.xlsx`](perfect-competition-model.xlsx), [`analysis/perfect-competition-analysis.md`](../../analysis/perfect-competition-analysis.md), [`docs/decisions/perfect-competition-memo.md`](../../docs/decisions/perfect-competition-memo.md)

Working through the perfect-competition case took me from a detailed brief, to a spec, to a finished spreadsheet built from that spec — a lot of trial and error, but I came out far more confident about blending my AI (actual intelligence/ideas) with AI (the artificial intelligence). It changed how I think about where AI fits into complex analysis, sharpened my troubleshooting, taught me to write hypotheses that are actually testable, and left me understanding Excel Solver far better than I did going in.
